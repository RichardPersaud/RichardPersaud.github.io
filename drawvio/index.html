Draw solo · Go live · Share

Draw live with friends.
Share artwork. Follow artists.

Drawvio is a real-time social drawing app: paint solo in a distraction-free studio, host live sessions with viewers and invited collaborators, chat while you draw, and publish to the feed.

See it in action Explore features

The app, in four frames

Every pixel of the interface stays out of the way of the art — an ink & paper chrome with a single vermilion accent for actions.

Made for drawing together

A drawing studio, a live stage, and a social gallery — one app, one seamless flow.

◎

Live sessions

Stream your canvas as you draw. Spectators watch in real time, invited collaborators can draw with you, and chat keeps the room talking.

✎

A serious studio

Pressure-smooth pen strokes, an eraser, rectangle/circle/line tools, and a curated palette — autosaved as you work.

❤

A social gallery

Publish finished pieces to the feed. Collect reactions, comments and bookmarks; follow artists so their work finds you.

➚

Notifications & activity

Know when someone reacts to your art, comments, starts following you, or opens a live session worth joining.

☼

Ink & paper design

A warm near-monochrome interface that never competes with the artwork — plus a full dark mode — and one accent for every action.

📧

No ads. Ever.

Drawvio shows no advertising and sells no data. The community server is run by the developer — see the privacy policy for details.

Built the artist-first way

Three principles shape every update.

Real time, not recorded time

Live sessions are true real-time collaboration — strokes land on every viewer's screen as they are drawn, backed by a realtime canvas layer.

Your art, your identity

You own what you draw. Publish to the feed, keep things as a live session for invited friends only, or keep a piece to yourself.

Quiet by design

No ads, no third-party trackers, no dark patterns. Moderation tools like blocking are built in, and the full terms are public.
