Deleting your PanaLix account

PanaLix for Android — account & data deletion instructions

To delete your PanaLix account and all data associated with it, send us an email and we will remove everything from our servers:

  1. Email richardpersaud2010@hotmail.com with the subject line “PanaLix account deletion request” (tap here to open it pre-filled).
  2. Include the Gmail address you use to sign in to PanaLix, so we can locate your account.
  3. Send it from that same Gmail address if possible — it lets us verify you faster.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in identity — the PanaLix account linked to your Google login
  • Account data — your account email and internal user ID
  • Cloud sync data — reading progress, chapters read, reading history, favorites/library, in-app settings, and your profile card (display name, avatar and background images)

What stays, and for how long

  • Anything stored only on your device (guest-mode data, caches) is deleted when you uninstall the app — we never had access to it.
  • Feedback messages you previously sent are kept for a short period for record-keeping, then deleted.
  • We keep no backups of your personal data beyond a short transition window needed to complete the deletion.

Heads up: deleting your account removes your cloud sync. If you keep using PanaLix afterwards, you can keep reading as a guest (nothing is collected) or create a fresh account by signing in again — it will start empty.

Requests are typically completed within 7 days. Questions about the process: richardpersaud2010@hotmail.com  ·  full details: Privacy Policy
