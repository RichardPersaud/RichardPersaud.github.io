Everything a night of anime needs

Built for binge-watching — the conveniences you'd expect, without the accounts, ads or clutter.

Skip intro, auto-next

HLS player with a quality selector (1080p → 360p), subtitles, skip-intro, and auto-play of the next episode.

Pick up where you left off

A Continue Watching row on Home and per-episode resume in the player, exactly where you paused.

Mini player

Dock the episode into a draggable corner mini player and keep browsing. Picture-in-picture on Android.

Browse everything

Full catalog with an A–Z index and filters for type, status, genre, season, score, and SUB/DUB.

Favorites & alerts

Heart any show and get notified the moment new episodes drop.

Cloud sync

Sign in with Google to sync favorites, history and settings across devices. Works fully offline without an account.

Everything streams through a local proxy — nothing leaves this machine except the requests to the source site.
