PanaLix Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

PanaLix is a manga reader app for Android published by Richard Persaud ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app and our services handle, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Guest use collects nothing. Without signing in, everything stays on your device.
  • Sign-in data. If you sign in with Google, we store your account email and a user ID.
  • Sync data. Signed-in users' reading progress, library, history, in-app settings, and profile card are synced so they follow you across devices.
  • Ads. PanaLix shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which processes certain device and usage data for advertising.
  • No data selling. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we handle

CategoryWhat it includesWhy
Account data Google account email, internal user ID To create and access your PanaLix account
Cloud sync data Reading progress, chapters read, reading history, favorites/library, app settings, display name, and profile card appearance (avatar and background images you choose) To sync your reading experience across your devices
Feedback The message you send; optional contact info you type in; app version To receive and respond to your feedback
Advertising data Device identifiers and other ad-related data handled by Google AdMob (see Google's disclosures below) To serve ads that keep the app free
Local device data All app state in guest mode; caches and preferences stored locally Functionality; never leaves your device unless you sync

Cloud services

For signed-in users, data is stored on Supabase (supabase.com), a third-party hosting service, in a dedicated project with row-level security: only your own authenticated account can read or change your data.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics beyond what is described here, and we do not sell or share it with anyone else.

Advertising

PanaLix uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google, as an independent controller of ad services, processes data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location, and interactions with ads. You can learn more and opt out of personalized advertising in Google Settings on your device (Settings → Google → Ads) and here:

  • How Google uses data from AdMob apps
  • Google Privacy Policy

Content sources

The app requests manga content from third-party online catalogs when you browse or read. These requests are made directly from your device for content delivery and are not combined with your personal information by us.

Data retention and deletion

  • Guest use: uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • Signed-in use: you can contact us to delete your account data, or delete your data yourself by clearing it in the app (for example, removing library items or history before syncing).
  • Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to act on them.

To request deletion of your account data, email richardpersaud2010@hotmail.com with the email address on your PanaLix account.

Children

PanaLix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

All sync traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access to your stored data is restricted to your authenticated account. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, announce the change in the app before (or when) it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: richardpersaud2010@hotmail.com
